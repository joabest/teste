What is ERP implementation?

ERP implementation is the process of selecting, configuring and launching an enterprise resource planning system like NetSuite, SAP, Odoo or Microsoft Dynamics, including data migration and training, usually led by an ERP implementation consultant. WeEvolveIT runs it in fixed-scope phases with consultants certified in SAP and NetSuite.

ERP implementation vs. ERP migration?
Implementation stands up your first ERP, replacing spreadsheets or basic tools. Migration moves you from one ERP to another and adds mapping and cleansing the old system's data.
On-premise vs. cloud ERP?
On-premise ERP runs on your servers: more control, more upkeep. Cloud ERP like NetSuite or Dynamics 365 is subscription-based: faster to deploy, lighter on IT.

Why ERP projects fail

Many ERP projects overrun or fail. Ours are built not to.

Why they fail

  • Scope creep: every department adds features and the timeline stretches.

  • Bad data: dirty records in, dirty reports out.

  • No change management: the system works, nobody adopts it.

  • Over-customization: custom code that breaks every upgrade.

  • Wrong-fit vendor: chosen for the demo, not your workflows.

  • Big-bang go-live: one failure halts the business.

FAQ

What teams ask before an ERP project.

01What are the phases of an ERP implementation?

A typical project runs through discovery, vendor selection, design, configuration, data migration, integrations, testing, training, go-live and support. We group these into our five phases with a fixed scope, so you see plan and cost up front and operations keep running.

02Why do ERP implementations fail?

The common causes are avoidable: runaway scope, dirty data migrated without cleansing, teams that never adopt the system, customization that breaks upgrades and big-bang go-lives. Our method answers each one from discovery, with fixed scope, clean data, built-in training and phased releases.

03How much does ERP implementation cost?

It depends on modules, users, integrations and data complexity, so there is no honest flat price. Senior consulting starts at $50 USD/hour, and a fixed-price discovery gives you a scoped number before any build. Support after go-live is hourly or a monthly retainer.

04Can we get fixed-price, phased ERP implementation?

Yes. Discovery is fixed-price and ends with a defined scope, phase plan and cost. The implementation is then priced up front for that scope and billed by go-live milestone, module by module. New scope becomes its own quoted phase instead of stretching the budget.

05How long does an ERP implementation take?

It depends on scope. A focused single-module rollout can go live in weeks; a full multi-module ERP for a mid-sized company usually takes a few months, in phases. We set the timeline in discovery, so you commit to a realistic plan.

06Which ERP is best for a small or mid-sized business: NetSuite, SAP, Odoo or Dynamics?

It depends on size, industry and budget. Odoo suits cost-conscious SMBs starting with a few modules; NetSuite, growing companies that want cloud finance; Dynamics 365, teams already on Microsoft; SAP, from Business One for smaller companies to S/4HANA for complex operations. We're vendor-neutral, so we recommend by fit, not by license.

07Are your ERP consultants certified?

Yes, in SAP and NetSuite: our senior consultants hold certifications in those two platforms. We also implement Odoo, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Acumatica as vendor-neutral consultants, choosing the platform by fit. We don't resell licenses, so no commission shapes the recommendation.

08Can we add AI to our existing ERP?

Yes, and without replacing it: AI works on top of your ERP. We connect AI agents to your current ERP and business data, under your company's control, to speed up analysis, quotations and repetitive processes on NetSuite, SAP, Odoo, Dynamics or Acumatica.
