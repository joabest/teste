What does a Google Ads agency do?

A Google Ads agency plans, builds and optimizes your pay-per-click (PPC) campaigns on Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) so ad spend turns into leads and sales, not just clicks. As a paid media agency, WeEvolveIT handles keywords, audiences, bids, creative, landing pages and tracking, and reports results in revenue and cost per acquisition.

PPC vs. SEO: what's the difference?
PPC buys visibility today and stops when the budget does. SEO earns rankings that keep working without paying per click. Used together, ads cover demand now while SEO builds underneath.

Key takeaways

  • Revenue, not vanity clicks: we optimize for ROAS and cost per acquisition, not impressions.

  • Your accounts, your data: the Google Ads and Meta accounts stay in your name, with no lock-in.

  • Flat fee, not % of spend: our fee doesn't grow with your ad spend.

  • Landing pages and tracking included: pages built to convert paid clicks, and tracking that ties spend to sales.

PPC red flags

Where ad spend leaks, and how we stop it.

Common pitfalls

  • Vanity metrics: clicks reported as success while sales stay flat.

  • Accounts in the agency's name: switch providers and lose your history.

  • Weak pages: paid clicks land on slow pages that don't convert.

  • No conversion tracking: spend without knowing what drives sales.

  • Set and forget: campaigns on autopilot, paying for the wrong keywords.

FAQ

What companies ask before hiring a PPC agency.

01What's included in your PPC management services?

Strategy, build and ongoing management of Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Display, YouTube) and Meta (Facebook, Instagram) campaigns, plus conversion tracking with GTM and GA4, landing pages and CRO, and reports on ROAS, cost per acquisition and revenue. The ad accounts stay in your name.

02How much does a Google Ads agency cost?

It depends on platforms, number of campaigns and scope. Some agencies charge a percentage of ad spend, others a fixed fee; we charge a fixed fee agreed up front, based on a rate that starts at $50 USD/hour. Ad spend is separate: you pay Google and Meta directly.

03Google Ads or Meta Ads: which is right for me?

Google Ads reaches people already searching for what you offer, so it captures high-intent demand. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) finds buyers by audience before they search, which suits awareness and retargeting. The mix depends on your margins, sales cycle and goals; many businesses use both.

04Do I own my Google Ads and Meta accounts?

Yes. Your Google Ads and Meta ad accounts, pixel, GA4 property and history stay in your name from day one; we work through the access you grant us. Some agencies run campaigns inside their own accounts, so the history leaves with them. With us, if you move on, everything stays with you.

05How do you measure paid media ROI?

We set up conversion tracking with Google Tag Manager and GA4 so each dollar ties to a lead or a sale, not a click. Reports show ROAS (return on ad spend), cost per acquisition and revenue by campaign, and we optimize toward profit.

06How do I choose a Google Ads agency?

Compare four things: who runs your account day to day, whether the ad accounts stay in your name, whether reports show revenue and cost per acquisition instead of clicks, and whether the PPC company charges a fixed fee or a percentage of spend. Be wary of guaranteed results before an audit.

07Can a nearshore PPC agency in Mexico manage US campaigns?

Yes. Our bilingual senior team runs English and Spanish campaigns for US and Mexican companies, from national US campaigns and Texas local targeting to all of Mexico. Monterrey is on UTC-6 all year: the same time as Dallas and Chicago from November to March, and one hour behind them from March to November.
