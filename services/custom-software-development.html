What is custom software development?

Custom software development is designing and building software around a company's own workflows, data and rules instead of adapting to off-the-shelf tools. It covers web and mobile apps, internal platforms, integrations and SaaS. WeEvolveIT, a custom software development company in Monterrey, Mexico, builds it with senior engineers.

Custom vs. off-the-shelf software?
Off-the-shelf software makes you adapt to it: generic features, recurring fees, fixed limits. Custom software fits your workflow and connects to the tools you already use. Buy for commodity needs; build when software is your edge.
Do I own the code and IP?
Yes, always. Source code, IP and data are yours from day one, with no lock-in: host it, extend it or move it anywhere.

FAQ

What teams ask before building custom software.

01What is custom software development?

Custom software development is planning, designing, building and maintaining software for one organization's users, processes and integrations. Unlike packaged products sold to many buyers, it is made for a single client, who usually owns the result: a web app, mobile app, internal platform or SaaS product.

02Is bespoke software development the same as custom software?

Yes. Bespoke software development is another name for custom software: an application made for one business instead of bought off the shelf. Our bespoke software development services run on our five-phase method, from discovery to evolution, with senior engineers on every build.

03What do your custom software development services include?

End to end: discovery and scoping, UX/UI and architecture, full-stack development for web, mobile and SaaS, APIs and integrations, AI features and post-launch improvements. We can also run the cloud, data and analytics around it, so one senior team replaces several vendors.

04How much does custom software development cost?

It depends on scope and complexity, so we price it after discovery. Choose a fixed-scope project with the price set up front, a dedicated team billed monthly per engineer, or staff augmentation from $50 USD an hour. Discovery turns your idea into a number you can plan around.

05How long does it take to build custom software?

A focused MVP takes weeks; a full platform takes months and keeps evolving after launch. We ship in increments, so you see working software every sprint instead of waiting for one big release. Discovery gives you a realistic timeline before the build starts.

06Can I hire dedicated custom software developers instead of a fixed project?

Yes. You can add senior custom software developers to your team through staff augmentation from $50 USD an hour, or hire a dedicated team billed monthly per engineer for long-term projects. They join your tools and routines, and everything they build belongs to you.

07Do you build custom financial, healthcare or logistics software?

Yes. We handle custom financial software development (payments, ledgers, dashboards), healthcare platforms such as patient portals and clinical tools, and logistics systems for tracking and routing. We also build for retail, insurance and manufacturing, starting every project by mapping your sector's rules.

08How do I choose a custom software development company?

Look for senior engineers who stay on your project, a contract that gives you the source code and IP, and real experience in your industry. Then check hours: our Monterrey team runs on UTC-6 all year, the same time as Dallas and Chicago from November to March and one hour behind them from March to November.
