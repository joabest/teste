What is legacy system modernization?

Legacy system modernization is updating outdated software, platforms and infrastructure, such as mainframes, old codebases and unsupported apps, into modern systems that are easier to maintain and integrate. WeEvolveIT does it in stages from Monterrey, Mexico: we wrap the old system in APIs and migrate piece by piece while the business keeps running.

What is a legacy system?
Software a business still relies on that is hard to maintain, change or connect, like a mainframe or an unsupported app.
Why do companies still use legacy systems?
They still work, they hold years of business rules and replacing them feels risky, so teams patch them instead.

FAQ

What teams ask before modernizing.

01What is legacy system modernization?

Legacy system modernization is moving a business off outdated software, platforms or infrastructure without losing what those systems do: assessing the old system, choosing an approach per component, connecting it through APIs and migrating in stages, so it gets cheaper to maintain and easier to change.

02What are the main legacy modernization approaches?

The 7 R's come down to three practical moves: rehost (move it as-is to modern infrastructure, the fastest relief), replatform or refactor (update the stack, keep the business logic) and rebuild (re-architect the parts worth it). We choose per component, never one approach for everything.

03Is it worth replacing a legacy system, or should we modernize it?

It depends on the component, so we decide it in the assessment. A full replacement is slow, costly and risky, and can lose years of business rules. Modernizing keeps what works and updates the rest in stages; we replace only where the numbers justify it.

04How much does it cost to modernize legacy software?

It depends on the size and complexity of the system and how much you rebuild. Senior engineering starts at $50 USD/hour. A fixed-scope assessment comes first and prices each stage of the roadmap before any change, so you can fund the work in phases.

05Can you modernize without replacing the whole system or stopping operations?

Yes. We wrap the legacy system in APIs so new apps and services use it through a stable boundary, then modernize behind it in stages, each validated and with a rollback plan. The old system keeps running and retires gradually, with no all-at-once cutover.

06Can AI help modernize legacy code?

Yes. AI reads and documents legacy code, even COBOL, so we know what the old system really does before changing it, and it speeds up translating that code. Senior engineers lead the work; AI assists but doesn't decide what to keep.

07What does a legacy system modernization services company do, and how do I choose one?

A legacy software modernization company assesses your old systems, picks an approach per component and migrates them in stages. Choose one that starts with an assessment, not a rewrite pitch, and staffs senior engineers. WeEvolveIT works this way, nearshore from Monterrey, Mexico: 1,000+ projects, 17 countries, 15+ years.
