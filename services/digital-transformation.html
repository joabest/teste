What is digital transformation consulting?

Digital transformation consulting helps a business rethink how it operates and delivers value with modern technology: at WeEvolveIT, senior consultants and engineers build the roadmap, then modernize systems, automate processes, and put data and AI to work.

Digital transformation vs. digitization?
Digitization turns paper into PDFs. Digital transformation goes further: it redesigns processes, systems, and business models around technology, instead of putting the old way on a screen.
Strategy consulting vs. doing the work?
Many consultancies stop at the roadmap. We advise and build, so the plan becomes running software, not shelfware.

FAQ

What teams ask before a transformation.

01What does a digital transformation consultant do?

A digital transformation consultant assesses how your business runs today, defines the target state, and builds a costed roadmap to reach it, change management included. At WeEvolveIT, the same team then delivers that roadmap: the cloud, data, AI, and software it calls for.

02Why do most digital transformations fail?

About 70% of digital transformations fall short of their objectives, according to BCG's 2020 study "Flipping the Odds of Digital Transformation Success." Common causes: strategy with no delivery, too much at once, weak change management, technology with no business outcome, and no measurement.

03How do you build a digital transformation roadmap?

Start with a fixed-scope discovery of operations, goals, systems, and data, and define the target state. Then rank initiatives by value and risk, quick wins first, and give each a cost, an owner, and a KPI. The result is a roadmap you can execute in phases.

04How much does digital transformation consulting cost?

Senior digital transformation consulting and engineering start at $50 USD/hour. The total depends on how many initiatives you take on and how deep the systems work goes, so we price a fixed-scope discovery first, then each phase against milestones before you commit.

05How do you choose a digital transformation consulting firm?

Pick a firm that can both plan and build, shows a costed roadmap before asking for a long commitment, ties each initiative to a KPI, and has worked with systems like yours. Ask for case studies, and ask who will actually do the work.

06IT transformation vs. digital transformation: what's the difference?

IT transformation modernizes the technology itself: infrastructure, core systems, and how IT delivers. Digital transformation is broader: it changes how the whole business operates and serves customers, and IT transformation is usually one part of it. Our consulting covers both, from cloud and core systems to process, data, and AI.

07Is digital transformation only for large companies?

No. Small and midsize businesses transform too; only the scope changes. Instead of a multi-year program, start with one initiative with a clear payback, such as automating a manual process or connecting scattered data, and expand once it proves its value.
