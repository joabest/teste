Retailers and e-commerce operators across categories — from general online stores and furniture retail to specialty verticals like mirrors. Also includes B2B hardware distribution. We help unify channels, fix conversion bottlenecks, and turn fulfillment from a cost center into a moat.

The pattern we keep seeing across this sector: technology rarely creates new problems — it amplifies the operational ones that were already there. The clients who win are the ones who let tooling expose the gaps, then close them deliberately rather than reactively.
